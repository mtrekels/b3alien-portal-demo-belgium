Methods

  1. Checklist. The national GRIIS checklist (ColDP format, ChecklistBank) is matched to the GBIF taxonomic backbone (Catalogue of Life eXtended Release) used by the occurrence cube. Synonyms resolve to the accepted species, infraspecific taxa to their species.
  2. Occurrence cube. A GBIF occurrence cube counts records per species, month and grid cell. The cell codes of the EEA reference grid are enough to draw the map: no GIS files are needed.
  3. Discoveries. For each checklist species, the first month with a GBIF record gives the year of discovery. The number of new species per year is the input of the model.
  4. Rate of establishment. The Solow & Costello (2004) model separates the rate of introduction from the probability of detection. Its parameter β₁ is the yearly trend of the introduction rate (eβ₁ − 1 = relative change per year). Uncertainty is estimated by bootstrapping residuals.
  5. Invasive species. The indicator is also computed for the subset of checklist species assessed as invasive in the checklist: flagged as invasive in the species profile, or with a national degree of establishment "invasive" or "widespread invasive". This subset is much smaller, so its confidence intervals are wider.
  6. Sampling effort. Observers and occurrences per year from the same cube document how detectability changed over time.

GBIF records reflect where and when people observed, not the true distribution: absence of records is not evidence of absence, and discoveries lag behind introductions.

    Integrate in a reporting portal

    Everything on this page is static: a few JSON files produced by a Python pipeline and plain JavaScript. A national portal can integrate the indicator in three ways:

    1. Embed a component with an iframe. Each block can be shown on its own with the embed parameter (indicators, map, cumulative, annual, effort, species). Add subset=invasive for the invasive species only. The map also accepts layer and basemap, and layer=species&species=<GBIF key>&color=year for one invasive species.
    2. Use the data files (summary.json, timeseries.json, species.json, invasive_species_cells.json, grid_10km.json, grid_1km.json) with the portal's own charting and mapping components.
    3. Re-run the pipeline for another country or a newer GBIF download; the page updates without code changes.
